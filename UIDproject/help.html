Answers

How do I cancel my booking?

Open your dashboard, choose the booking and select Cancel. Refund amounts depend on how close you are to departure.

What is the refund policy?

Refunds are returned to your original payment method. Earlier cancellations receive a larger refund.

Can I change my cabin after booking?

Yes, subject to availability. Price differences are shown before you confirm.

Is travel insurance included?

It is optional. You can add it from the payment summary.

What documents are required for boarding?

A valid photo ID that matches your booking, uploaded at registration. International sailings need a passport.

Can I customize my itinerary after booking?

Yes. Use the Itinerary Planner to add or remove activities and meals.
